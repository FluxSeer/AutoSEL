# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AutoSEL a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of
experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, empathetic, constructive, and accountable. Focus feedback on
the work and its safety or correctness rather than on individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct,
trolling, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and
handled fairly and confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
